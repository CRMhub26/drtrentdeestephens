Dr. Trent Dee Stephens is an accomplished author and educator whose published works explore a wide range of subjects, including anatomy, embryology, science, religion, medieval history, and historical fiction. His writing reflects decades of research, teaching, and intellectual curiosity.

About Dr. Trent Dee Stephens

Dr. Stephens earned BS degrees in Microbiology and Zoology and an MS in Zoology from Brigham Young University, then a PhD in Anatomy from the University of Pennsylvania. After postdoctoral training at the University of Washington, he joined Idaho State University in 1981, where he taught Anatomy and Developmental Biology for approximately 30 years.
